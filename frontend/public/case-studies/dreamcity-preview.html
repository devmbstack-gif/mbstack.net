Who we are

DreamCity Capital digital real-estate investment platform offers investment opportunities in pre-vetted real estate assets and under a model that streamlines stakes ownership and ensures transparent and inclusive management of our collective stakeholder's interest.

Our Investment Offerings

Our investment offerings are opportunities to acquire equity ownership interests in pre-vetted and partly funded real estate assets expertly managed under a special purpose company administered by DreamCity Capital.

Streamlined Stake Ownership

Investors are issued shares on the company (SPC) proportionate to their invested stakes in the asset(s), thus representing their expected earnings from the investment assets.

Our Investment Category

Our investment categories are diversified and meant to offer equity ownership on the administered company, fractional ownership and fully ownership right over the specific real estate assets.
